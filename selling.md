# Selling PulseTeam

## The pitch
PulseTeam helps remote teams spot burnout and disengagement early — without surveillance.

## Key points
- Privacy-first: no content capture, no keystrokes
- Team-level insights, not individual monitoring
- Wellness nudges that actually help
- Built for distributed teams

## What you're selling
1. Working MVP and demo
2. Product spec and design
3. Codebase (ready to continue)
4. Customer acquisition roadmap
5. Privacy-compliant design

## Target buyers
- Founders of HR-tech SaaS
- Remote-first company CTOs
- People-ops leaders
- Startup accelerators
